Prompt Engineering Courses

There are many free courses and many paid courses.

Free Courses

Craft Most Effective AI Prompts To Get The Most Out Of ChatGPT is a good place to start. It is useful for all vitual assistants, not just ChatGPT.

Coursera

Coursera has very many courses on very many subjects, including many relevant courses. The following are available for free.

  • Essentials of Prompt Engineering (from AWS; short; good introduction to Prompt Engineering)
  • Generative AI: Prompt Engineering Basics (from IBM) is quite good. There are various labs that will open in a new browser tab or window. After a lab, close the lab tab or wiindow. It is not obvious how to proceed after a lab. If there is no obvious way to proceed then click on or tap the next lesson in the left sidebar.
  • Prompt Engineering Masterclass - From Beginner to Advanced (from Packt)
  • Prompt Engineering Specialization (from Vanderbilt University)
  • Prompt Engineering Generative AI & LLM Models Fundamentals (from Whizlabs)
  • Prompt Engineering for LLMs (from Edureka)
  • Generative AI and Prompt Engineering Essentials (from Edureka)
