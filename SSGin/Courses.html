  • Generative AI: Prompt Engineering Basics from Board Infinity is worth trying but might be difficult to follow.
  • Generative AI: Prompt Engineering Basics (from IBM)
  • Prompt Engineering Masterclass - From Beginner to Advanced (from Packt)
  • Prompt Engineering Specialization (from Vanderbilt University)
  • Prompt Engineering Generative AI & LLM Models Fundamentals (from Whizlabs)
  • Prompt Engineering for LLMs (from Edureka)
  • Generative AI and Prompt Engineering Essentials (from Edureka)
